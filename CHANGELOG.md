## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

### New features

- Book QuadX, SPX and LBC shipments by hand, or let a connected store send them in.
- See every shipment in one list, with a Needs action view for the ones waiting on you and a search by name, order or tracking number.
- Book, cancel and print waybills for many shipments at once.
- Follow each parcel with live courier status and a tracking link.
- Save pickup addresses, pick a default one, and keep a list of customers to fill in a shipment faster.
- Set up each courier account with Sandbox and Production keys, and check them with Save and test.
- Connect your stores with API keys and webhooks, and see a log of every webhook sent.
- Check whether a courier delivers to an address and get a shipping quote, with a flag when the address is outside the delivery zone.
- Switch between light and dark mode, or follow your device.
- Use Ventaship on your phone as well as your computer.
- Change your name, email and password from your account page.
